World Cup 2026

Who should you root for?

A title lifts a country's mood. The lift is bigger where more people care, where people have less, and where the joy lingers because a win would be a first rather than a habit. By default this ranks all 48 teams by the joy of beating expectations: a deep run counts for more the less it was expected, and the more it was a long shot. Win probability sets the bar a team is measured against. It never shrinks the score, so underdogs are rewarded, not penalized.
